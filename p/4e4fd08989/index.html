OUR MEETING / 00

What we heard

  1. Timing matters more than anything
    You are moving to the US early in the new year, and the home should not sit empty for six to twelve months while you carry two mortgages. Every step below is built around selling in that window.
  2. The home was kept too quiet
    Previous marketing was discreet, slow to go wide, and buyers simply could not find it. You want it seen, openly, by the right audience.
  3. You are not expecting a buyer in our pocket
    You told me you want honest updates and real activity, not promises. That is exactly how we work: a report every two weeks, even when there is nothing new.
  4. The offers so far came with strings
    Part cash, payment spread over years, a partner who wanted to flip it. We qualify every buyer before they walk through your door, so your time goes only to serious, financed people.
  5. Exclusivity should not mean shutting others out
    You do not want an agent who uses your home to generate leads for themselves. Our exclusivity is open: any agent with a real buyer is welcome, and we coordinate it for you.

SCOPE / 02

What is included

  1. A full video production with drone, in four cuts
    A complete tour for buyers abroad, a classic cut, a bolder social cut and a short teaser, released every week or two. A buyer overseas decides to fly in based on video.
  2. Professional stills, inside and from the air
    The images travel with the home through every channel and every conversation, and set the level of the price before anyone mentions a number.
  3. A written listing in English and Hebrew, with the floor plan
    Not a list of square meters. A story of three levels, the terrace that runs along the sea and the pool on the roof, written for each audience in its own language.
  4. Release across all Davidson channels and our mailing list
    Immediate exposure to an international audience that is already looking at Netanya, from day one.
  5. Direct outreach to matched buyers from our database
    We have active buyers in this range. Each one who fits receives a personal approach and a follow-up, not a mass message.
  6. A background check before every viewing
    No time-wasters and no curious neighbours. Only qualified buyers come through your door, at times that suit you.
  7. Open cooperation with agents in Israel and abroad
    Any agent with a serious buyer, including the friends who have shown the home before, is welcome. We coordinate it so you deal with one point of contact.
  8. Viewings and negotiation through to signing
    You do not answer callers, chase agents or argue price with a buyer. We do that, and bring you every serious offer to decide on.
  9. A progress report every two weeks, and a shared WhatsApp group
    Views, enquiries, viewings and what buyers actually said about the home, in their own words. You, Warren, Amir and me in one group, so nothing waits for a phone call.
  10. A plan B that does not slow the sale
    If you decide to let the home while it is on the market, we can sell with a tenant in place and coordinate viewings around the tenancy. The sale stays the priority.

TERMS / 05

Terms

  1. Exclusivity, three months, with open cooperation. For three months the home is marketed through Davidson Real Estate. That is what lets us invest the full production and take responsibility for the whole process. Exclusivity here does not close the door: any agent in Israel or abroad with a real buyer is welcome to bring them, coordinated through us.
  2. Sales commission. 2% + VAT of the actual sale price, payable on signing of the sale contract.
  3. Production. Our standard production fee is ₪18,000 + VAT. Under this exclusivity the production is fully on us, and you pay nothing for it.
  4. Asking price and honest expectations. We go to market at ₪23,000,000. Aiming high is the only way to leave room to negotiate, and we will say the truth in the same breath: the penthouse at Ehud Manor 18, 420 sqm, has been asking ₪16,250,000 since January 2026 and was still unsold this month, and a closed penthouse sale nearby, at Tzukei HaShmura in November 2024, was a little above ₪18,000,000. Your home has what they do not: three levels, a terrace that runs along the sea, a pool on the roof and a private lift. That is the case we will make, and we will fight for the highest price the market will carry.
  5. A calibration point agreed in advance. After the first month on the market or after eight viewings, whichever comes first, we sit down together with the data and the feedback and decide whether to adjust. Price moves are decided by what buyers say, not by us, and there is no substitute for hearing it from them.
  6. Protecting the price on the phone. Anyone who calls and asks about flexibility before seeing the home hears one answer: come and see it first, then we talk. We handle the first round of negotiation, and when an offer is serious enough, it comes to you to decide.
  7. Transparency. Every two weeks you receive the same report: what we did, how many views and from where, how many enquiries and viewings, and what buyers said about the home in their own words.
  8. Validity. Fourteen days from the date of issue. After that we will gladly update the terms to our production schedule.
